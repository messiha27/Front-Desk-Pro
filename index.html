Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is Front Desk Pro for?

Dental practice owners, office managers, and front desk teams who want a more consistent approach to training and everyday workflows.

Can we add our own office procedures?

The planned manager tools allow practices to add approved scripts, policies, checklists, and training materials alongside the core curriculum.

Does it replace dental software or clinical training?

No. It is an administrative training resource. It does not replace practice-management software, payer-specific verification, clinical judgment, or your practice's approved procedures.

Can we use patient information in training?

Use fictional examples or properly de-identified scenarios only. Do not enter patient names, dates of birth, clinical notes, insurance IDs, or other protected health information into training content.

How will billing work?

The intended model is a recurring monthly or annual subscription. Billing terms, renewal, cancellation, and refund details will be shown before purchase when subscriptions launch.

Can we try it before subscribing?

We plan to work with a small number of pilot practices to test the training experience and improve the product before a wider launch.
